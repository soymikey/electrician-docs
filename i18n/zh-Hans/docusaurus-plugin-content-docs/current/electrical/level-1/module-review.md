电工这一行靠的是会干活的人。他们安装和维护住宅、商业、工业的电气系统与设备。这一模块讲电工有哪些职业路径、怎样成为电工学徒，以及员工和雇主各自要负什么责任。

电工安全。学完要能在指导下选用 PPE，并按一项具体任务说出安全要求。

当电工有危险。要随时留意会伤人的东西，并遵守基本安全规则。这一模块讲用电的危险、用来保护人的个人防护装备（PPE），以及和电气安全有关的规定。最后还会学职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）的上锁挂牌（lockout/tagout, LOTO）规则。

> Being an electrician can be a dangerous job. It is important to stay mindful of things that can cause harm and to follow basic safety rules. In this module, you will learn about the dangers of working with electricity. You will also learn about the types of personal protective equipment (PPE) used to keep people safe and the rules related to electrical safety. 电看不见，但危险很大。电工必须靠测试仪表可靠地确认有没有电。系统运行时的性能和特性，也只能用可靠的仪表测出来，很多计算都依赖测得准。这一模块介绍常用电气测试仪表，以及每种仪表怎样安全地测量电气系统的特性。

## 26113-26 Electrical Theory and Circuits

电气理论与电路。本模块没有实操任务，以知识为主。

### Overview

要成为熟练电工，下一步是弄懂电气理论和那些看不见的力。这一模块讲基本原子理论，以及电能怎样在导体里移动。你还会学可以测量的电气特性，以及对应的单位。电工做的所有工作，都是在电源和用电点之间做出安全路径，把电能管住、隔住。第一步就是弄懂电是怎样流动的。

> Understanding electrical theory and the invisible forces at work is the next step in your journey toward becoming a skilled electrician. This module explains basic atomic theory and how electrical energy moves through conductors. You will also learn about measurable electrical characteristics and the related units of measure. As an electrician, all the work you do is related to the management and containment of electrical energy by creating safe paths between the power source and the point of consumption. The first step is understanding how electricity moves.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 描述原子结构，并说明电怎样在材料里移动
  - Describe atomic structure and explain how electricity moves through materials.
  - [ ] a. 指出原子的组成部分，并说明电流怎样流动

  - [ ] b. 识别并描述导体和绝缘体
    - Identify and describe electrical conductors and insulators.
  - [ ] c. 描述电磁，以及它怎样用在电气设备上
    - Describe electromagnetism and how it applies to electrical devices.

- [ ] **2.** 定义电路里可以测量的特性，并用欧姆定律完成计算
  - Define measurable electrical circuit characteristics and complete calculations using Ohm’s law.
  - [ ] a. 定义常见的电路量
    - Define common electrical circuit values.
  - [ ] b. 说明怎样用欧姆定律求出未知的电路量
    - Explain how to use Ohm’s law to determine unknown circuit values.
  - [ ] c. 说明电功率怎样测量和计算
    - Explain how electrical power is measured and calculated.

- [ ] **3.** 识别电路类型，计算相关电气量，并认出常见图纸符号
  - Identify types of electrical circuits, calculate related electrical values, and recognize common drawing symbols.
  - [ ] a. 识别串联电路和并联电路，并计算电气量

> Have you ever seen a site plan? How about an electrical drawing? These are both construction documents. Approved construction plans include these documents as well as project specifications, such as floor plans, detail drawings, lighting plans, power riser diagrams, and schedules. ## 26115-26 Conduit Preparation and Bending

导管加工与弯曲。学完要能切割、铰口、套丝，并用手动或机械弯管器做出 90° 弯、偏移弯和鞍形弯。

### Overview

各种导管安装时经常要改变方向：终端处简单的偏移，到柱子、梁这类障碍处的角度偏移和鞍形弯，都有。多数情况下，弯管之前还要先切割、铰口、套丝。这一模块训练导管的加工和弯曲。弯管的数学原理对各种弯管器都适用，但每种弯管器有自己的特点和能力。训练和练习能帮你用不同工具把导管加工做熟。

> Installing conduit of all types often requires many changes of direction, ranging from simple offsets at the point of termination to angular offsets and saddles at columns, beams, and similar obstructions. In most cases, conduit must be cut, reamed, and threaded prior to bending.
>
> This module provides training in the preparation and bending of conduit. Although the mathematical principles of bending apply to all types of benders, each type of bender has its own unique characteristics and capabilities. Training and practice will help you develop the necessary skills to master conduit fabrication using a variety of tools.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 说明怎样切割、铰口和套丝金属导管，以及怎样加工聚氯乙烯（PVC）导管
  - Explain how to cut, ream, and thread metal conduit and how to work with polyvinyl chloride (PVC) conduit.
  - [ ] a. 描述怎样切割和铰口金属导管
    - Describe how to cut and ream metal conduit.
  - [ ] b. 描述怎样给金属导管套丝
    - Describe how to thread metal conduit.

- [ ] **2.** 识别 NEC 对导管弯曲的要求
  - Identify the NEC® requirements for conduit bends.
  - [ ] a. 识别导管的最小弯曲半径
    - Identify the minimum bend radius for conduit.
  - [ ] b. 说出单段导管里最多可以有几个弯
    - State how many bends can be placed in a single conduit run.

- [ ] **3.** 把数学用到弯管上
  - Apply mathematics to conduit bending.
  - [ ] a. 解和弯管有关的三角函数和方程
    - Solve trigonometric functions and equations related to conduit bending.
