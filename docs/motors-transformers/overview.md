# Motors and Transformers

Motors and transformers both rely on magnetic fields and changing current. Transformers transfer energy between windings. Motors convert electrical energy into mechanical motion.

## What to connect

- AC systems naturally create changing magnetic fields.
- Transformers use primary and secondary windings to change voltage and current relationships.
- Motors use magnetic interaction to create torque.
- Back EMF, inrush current, and load conditions matter when thinking about motors.
