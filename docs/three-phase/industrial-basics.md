# Three-Phase and Industrial Basics

Three-phase power uses three AC waveforms offset from each other. It is common in commercial and industrial settings because it delivers smoother power and works well with motors.

## Key ideas

- Line voltage and phase voltage are not always the same.
- Balanced three-phase loads are easier to calculate and operate.
- Power factor matters because apparent power and real power differ in AC systems.
- VFDs control motor speed by changing frequency and voltage behavior.
- Star-delta starters reduce starting current for some motor applications.
