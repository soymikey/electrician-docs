## 03101 Introduction to HVACR

HVACR 入门。本模块没有实操任务，以知识为主。

### Overview

全世界有数以百万计的住宅和商业建筑装有供暖系统。其中很大一部分还有舒适制冷，多数还以某种形式带有制冷设备。商店和餐馆里也有大量制冷设备。

受过 HVACR 培训的技术工人，可以在新建工程里安装系统，也可以在已有设施里维修设备。这个行业一直在增长、一直在变，愿意学习和练技能的人机会很多。本模块是进入这一行的起点。

> Many millions of homes and businesses across the world have a heating system. A large percentage of them have comfort cooling systems, and the majority of them also contain a refrigeration system in one form or another. Millions of stores and restaurants also house a great deal of refrigeration equipment.
>
> Craftworkers trained in the HVACR industry have the opportunity to install systems in new construction and service equipment in existing facilities. The HVACR craft is constantly growing and changing, offering endless opportunities for those willing to learn and develop the necessary skills. This module represents the starting point of your journey into an exciting craft.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 陈述供暖、通风、空调和制冷的基本原理
  - State the basic principles of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration.
  - [ ] a. 陈述供暖的基本原理
    - State the basic principles of heating.
  - [ ] b. 陈述通风的基本原理
    - State the basic principles of ventilation.
  - [ ] c. 陈述空调的基本原理
    - State the basic principles of air conditioning.
  - [ ] d. 陈述制冷的基本原理
    - State the basic principles of refrigeration.

- [ ] **2.** 描述指导 HVACR 安装和维修的原则
  - Describe the principles that guide HVACR installation and service techniques.
  - [ ] a. 识别常见的 HVACR 安全问题和安全要求
    - Identify some common HVACR safety concerns and guidelines.
  - [ ] b. 描述技术员在系统节能运行中的作用，并说明 LEED 原则怎样影响绿色建筑的设计和施工
    - Describe the role of HVACR technicians in energy-efficient system operation and state how LEED principles affect green-building design and construction.
  - [ ] c. 描述常见的承包商和技术员执照要求
    - Describe common HVACR contractor and technician licensure requirements.
  - [ ] d. 识别重要的规范（codes）和许可证（permits）
    - Identify important codes and permits.

- [ ] **3.** 识别 HVACR 的职业路径，并描述常见培训过程
  - Identify HVACR career paths available and describe common training processes.
  - [ ] a. 识别成为合格技术工人所需的个人特质
    - Identify the personal characteristics needed to be a successful craft professional.
  - [ ] b. 描述 HVACR 各业务板块的职业机会
    - Describe career opportunities in the various business segments of the HVACR industry.
  - [ ] c. 描述培训怎样开展，以及学徒制的一般要求和结构
    - Describe how HVACR training is delivered and the general requirements and structure of an apprenticeship program.

行业数学。本模块没有实操任务，以知识为主。切管、配管、风管选型和算电流都会用到。

数学是在 HVACR 这一行里往上走必须会的技能。切管、配管、风管选型和安装，以及计算电流这类电气数值，都会用到数学。

> Math is an essential skill required to advance in the HVACR profession. Math is used when cutting and fitting pipe, when sizing and installing ductwork, and when calculating electrical values such as current flow.

## 03106 Basic Electricity

基础电学。学完要能在指导下画出并组装一个带控制变压器和继电器的电路，再测通断、电阻、电流和电压，并确认电源已经断开。

### Overview

HVACR 系统要有电源才能运行。技术员接到维修电话时，多数问题都和电气系统有关，所以必须懂电气理论、动力元件和控制元件，以及电路。这些是入行的基础，用来看懂复杂系统的电路，并在出问题时排查。

> An HVACR system needs an electrical power source to operate. Most of the problems an HVACR technician encounters when faced with a service call involve the electrical system, so an understanding of electrical theory, power and control components, and circuits is essential. Technicians must have a firm foundation in these topics to get started in the craft, allowing them to understand the circuits of complex systems and troubleshoot them when problems arise.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 陈述发电的基本原理，并识别常见电气安全做法
  - State the fundamentals of power generation and identify common electrical safety practices.
  - [ ] a. 说明电力怎样产生和输送，并识别产生的两种电流
    - State how electrical power is generated and distributed and identify the two kinds of current produced.
  - [ ] b. 识别一般电气安全做法，并陈述电气上锁挂牌（lockout/tagout）程序
    - Identify general electrical safety practices and state electrical lockout/tagout procedures.
  - [ ] c. 陈述 OSHA 电气安全要求，并识别常见电气个人防护装备（PPE）
    - State OSHA electrical safety requirements and identify common electrical PPE.

- [ ] **2.** 解释基本电气理论
  - Explain basic electrical theory.
  - [ ] a. 定义常用电气单位，并应用欧姆定律和功率公式
    - Define common electrical units and apply Ohm’s law and the power formula.
  - [ ] b. 描述串联电路和并联电路的区别，并计算每种电路的数值
    - Describe the differences between series and parallel circuits and calculate circuit values for each type.

- [ ] **3.** 识别 HVACR 工作中用的电气测量仪表，并描述怎样使用
  - Identify the electrical measuring instruments used in HVACR work and describe how to use them.
  - [ ] a. 描述怎样测量电压
    - Describe how voltage is measured.
  - [ ] b. 描述怎样测量电流
    - Describe how current is measured.
  - [ ] c. 描述怎样测量电阻，以及怎样判断通断（continuity）
    - Describe how resistance is measured and how continuity is determined.

- [ ] **4.** 识别 HVACR 系统中的电气元件，并描述它们的功能
  - Identify electrical components used in HVACR systems and describe their functions.
  - [ ] a. 识别并描述各种负载装置，并说明它们在电路图上怎样表示
    - Identify and describe various load devices and explain how they are represented on circuit diagrams.
  - [ ] b. 识别并描述各种控制装置，并说明它们在电路图上怎样表示
    - Identify and describe various control devices and explain how they are represented on circuit diagrams.
  - [ ] c. 识别并描述 HVACR 工作中常见的电气图
    - Identify and describe common electrical diagrams used in HVACR work.

## 03108 Introduction to Heating

供暖入门。学完要能在指导下认出诱导通风炉或冷凝炉的主要部件，记录温升、歧管燃气压力和火焰质量，并按厂家说明书核对安装间距。

### Overview

多数住宅和企业都需要某种供暖系统。安装和维修炉子责任很大。因为经常涉及明火和可燃燃料，始终存在火灾或爆炸的可能。训练有素的技术员能把这些危险降到最低；化石燃料也一直被用来安全、可靠地给成千上万栋建筑供暖。

选择哪一种供暖系统，都有实际理由。多数供暖系统只要按厂家说明书正确安装，并由合格技术员维护，就能满意地运行很多年。

> Most homes and businesses need some type of heating system. Installing and servicing furnaces is a big responsibility. Because flames and combustible fuels are often involved, there is always the potential for fire or explosion. A well-trained technician minimizes those hazards, and fossil fuels are consistently used to heat many thousands of buildings safely and reliably.
>
> There are practical reasons for choosing one heating system over another. When most heating systems are properly installed according to the manufacturer’s instructions and serviced by qualified technicians, they will operate satisfactorily for many years.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 解释供暖和燃烧的基本概念
  - Explain the fundamental concepts of heating and combustion.
  - [ ] a. 描述传热过程，以及温度、湿度和热量怎样测量
    - Describe the heat transfer process and how temperature, humidity, and heat are measured.
  - [ ] b. 描述燃烧过程，并识别常见燃料及其特性
    - Describe the combustion process and identify common fuels and their characteristics.

- [ ] **2.** 识别并描述燃气炉、其部件，以及基本安装和维护要求
  - Identify and describe gas furnaces, their components, and their basic installation and maintenance requirements.
  - [ ] a. 描述各类燃气炉
    - Describe various types of gas furnaces.
  - [ ] b. 识别并描述常见燃气炉部件和控制装置
    - Identify and describe common gas furnace components and controls.
  - [ ] c. 描述燃气炉的基本安装和维护要求
    - Describe the basic installation and maintenance requirements of gas furnaces.

- [ ] **3.** 描述水暖（hydronic）和电暖系统
  - Describe hydronic and electric heating systems.
  - [ ] a. 描述水暖供暖系统，并识别常见部件
    - Describe hydronic heating systems and identify common components.
  - [ ] b. 描述电暖设备，并识别常见部件
    - Describe electric heating equipment and identify common components.

## 03107 Introduction to Cooling

制冷入门。学完要能在指导下测送风和回风的干球、湿球温度，接压力表组并计算过冷度（subcooling）和过热度（superheat），按钢瓶颜色识别制冷剂，并认出压缩机、冷凝器、蒸发器、节流装置和附件。

### Overview

要维修制冷系统和热泵，必须清楚理解制冷循环，以及主要部件各自做什么。这些知识适用于所有制冷剂回路。制冷剂和用途差别很多，这里讲的基本原理适用于所有直接膨胀（direct-expansion）制冷剂回路。

> To service cooling and heat pump systems, you must have a clear understanding of the refrigeration cycle and the function of its primary components. Technicians can apply this knowledge to all refrigerant circuits. Despite the differences in the many possible refrigerants and applications, the basic principles presented here apply to all direct-expansion refrigerant circuits.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 解释制冷循环的基本概念
  - Explain the fundamental concepts of the refrigeration cycle.
  - [ ] a. 描述传热与物质状态之间的关系
    - Describe the relationship between heat transfer and the state of substances.
  - [ ] b. 描述压力与温度的关系
    - Describe pressure/temperature relationships.
  - [ ] c. 描述制冷剂的基本流动，以及制冷循环中发生的状态变化
    - Describe basic refrigerant flow and the changes of state occurring in the refrigeration cycle.
  - [ ] d. 识别用来测量压力和温度的常用仪表
    - Identify common instruments used to measure pressure and temperature.

- [ ] **2.** 识别常见制冷剂及其识别特征
  - Identify common refrigerants and their identifying characteristics.
  - [ ] a. 识别氟碳制冷剂
    - Identify fluorocarbon refrigerants.
  - [ ] b. 描述氨作为制冷剂的用途
    - Describe the use of ammonia as a refrigerant.
  - [ ] c. 识别各类制冷剂容器及其安全操作要求
    - Identify various refrigerant containers and their safe handling requirements.

- [ ] **3.** 识别制冷系统的主要部件及其功能
  - Identify the major components of cooling systems and their function.
  - [ ] a. 识别各类压缩机
    - Identify various types of compressors.
  - [ ] b. 识别不同类型的冷凝器
    - Identify different types of condensers.
  - [ ] c. 识别不同类型的蒸发器
    - Identify different types of evaporators.
  - [ ] d. 描述用来计量制冷剂流量的装置
    - Describe devices used to meter refrigerant flow.
  - [ ] e. 说明制冷剂配管的基本概念
    - Recount basic refrigerant piping concepts.
  - [ ] f. 识别制冷剂回路中使用的各类附件
    - Identify various accessories used in refrigerant circuits.

- [ ] **4.** 识别制冷系统中常用的控制装置及其工作方式
  - Identify the common controls used in cooling systems and how they function.
  - [ ] a. 识别常见的主控制（primary controls）
    - Identify common primary controls.
  - [ ] b. 识别常见的辅助控制（secondary controls）
    - Identify common secondary controls.

## 03109 Air Distribution Systems

空气分配系统。学完要能在指导下用压力计（manometer）测风管内静压，用风速仪（velometer）测送风口风速，并据此计算风管内风量。

### Overview

HVAC 系统用风管把调节后的空气送到要制冷或供暖的空间。风管可以用薄金属板、玻璃纤维风管板或织物做成。系统表现好不好，和空气分配系统密切相关。风管必须尺寸和类型合适，并且安装、密封正确。技术员要明白空气分配系统的缺陷怎样影响性能，以及怎样认出这些缺陷才是问题的来源。

> HVAC systems use ductwork to deliver conditioned air to the spaces being cooled or heated. The ductwork is made of sheet metal, fiberglass ductboard, or fabric. The performance of any HVAC system is closely related to the performance of the air distribution system. The ductwork must be of the proper size and type, and it must be correctly installed and sealed. It is important for an HVAC technician to understand how deficiencies in an air distribution system affect performance and how to recognize these deficiencies as the source of a problem.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 描述空气分配系统中与空气流动及其测量有关的因素
  - Describe the factors related to air movement and its measurement in air distribution systems.
  - [ ] a. 描述气流中压力、速度和风量之间怎样相互关联
    - Describe how pressure, velocity, and volume are interrelated in airflow.
  - [ ] b. 描述典型住宅系统中的空气分配
    - Describe air distribution in a typical residential system.
  - [ ] c. 识别常用空气测量仪表
    - Identify common air measurement instruments.

- [ ] **2.** 描述构成空气分配系统的机械设备和材料
  - Describe the mechanical equipment and materials used to create air distribution systems.
  - [ ] a. 描述各类风机（blower）及其用途
    - Describe various blower types and applications.
  - [ ] b. 描述各类风扇设计及其用途
    - Describe various fan designs and applications.
  - [ ] c. 说明对风机定律（fan laws）的理解
    - Demonstrate an understanding of the fan laws.
  - [ ] d. 描述常见风管材料和管件
    - Describe common duct materials and fittings.
  - [ ] e. 识别常见格栅（grille）、送风口（register）和风阀（damper）的特性
    - Identify the characteristics of common grilles, registers, and dampers.

- [ ] **3.** 识别空气分配系统设计和节能的不同做法
  - Identify the different approaches to air distribution system design and energy conservation.
  - [ ] a. 识别各种空气分配系统的布置方式
    - Identify various air distribution system layouts.
  - [ ] b. 描述不同设计所形成的供暖和制冷气流
    - Describe heating and cooling air movement resulting from various air distribution system designs.
  - [ ] c. 说明怎样通过正确密封和测试空气分配系统来尽量提高能效
    - Explain how to maximize energy efficiency through the proper sealing and testing of air distribution systems.

## 03103 Basic Copper and Plastic Piping Practices

铜管和塑料管。学完要能在指导下切割并弯曲铜管，用扩口、卡套和压接接头连接铜管，并切割、连接 PVC 管和管件。

### Overview

铜管在 HVACR 工作中用得非常多，几乎所有常见制冷剂配管都以铜为基材。塑料管在供暖和制冷系统里也有多种用途：可以输送大型制冷系统的冷冻水、水冷系统的冷凝器水，也可以给高效燃气炉排烟。本模块介绍铜管和塑料管材料，并说明这些管路怎样应用和组装。

> Copper tubing is used extensively in HVACR work. Copper is the base material for virtually all common refrigerant piping. Plastic piping is also used in heating and cooling systems for a variety of purposes. Plastic piping may be used to carry chilled water for large cooling systems, condenser water for water-cooled mechanical systems, or even to vent high-efficiency gas furnaces. This module will introduce copper and plastic piping materials and explain how these piping systems are applied and assembled.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 识别不同类型的铜管及相关管件
  - Recognize different types of copper tubing and the related fittings.
  - [ ] a. 识别并描述铜管产品的特性
    - Identify and describe the characteristics of copper tubing products.
  - [ ] b. 识别各种铜管件
    - Identify various copper fittings.

- [ ] **2.** 说明怎样用机械方式连接铜管
  - Explain how to mechanically join copper tubing.
  - [ ] a. 说明怎样测量、切割、弯曲和胀口（swage）铜管
    - Explain how to measure, cut, bend, and swage copper tubing.
  - [ ] b. 描述怎样用扩口（flare）接头和卡套（compression）接头连接铜管
    - Describe how to join copper tubing using flare and compression joints.
  - [ ] c. 描述怎样用压接（press-to-connect）和推接（push-to-connect）接头连接铜管
    - Describe how to join copper tubing using press-to-connect and push-to-connect fittings.
  - [ ] d. 说明制冷剂管路怎样做压力试验
    - Explain how pressure testing on refrigerant lines is conducted.
  - [ ] e. 识别铜管安装中常见的吊架和支架
    - Identify common hangers and supports associated with copper tubing installations.

- [ ] **3.** 识别不同类型的塑料管，并说明它们怎样连接
  - Recognize different types of plastic piping and explain how they are joined.
  - [ ] a. 识别不同类型的塑料管
    - Recognize and identify different types of plastic piping.
  - [ ] b. 说明怎样连接各类塑料管
    - Explain how to join various types of plastic piping.

## 03104 Soldering and Brazing

锡焊和钎焊。学完要能在指导下正确启停空气-乙炔焊炬和氧乙炔设备，在不同平面用不同管件安全地锡焊和钎焊铜管（钎焊时用氮气吹扫），并把铜管钎焊到钢件或黄铜件上。

### Overview

在这一行里，锡焊（soldering）主要用于连接铜制水管和冷凝水管。制冷剂管路需要机械强度高、耐压的接头时，用钎焊（brazing）。两者都涉及可燃气体和明火，必须特别注意安全。经过一些练习，技术员可以较快掌握这两种技术。

> Soldering is used primarily to join copper water lines and condensate lines in the HVACR craft. Brazing is used when mechanically strong, pressure-resistant joints are needed for refrigerant lines. Both soldering and brazing demand careful attention to safety due to the hazards associated with flammable gases and open flames. With some practice, HVACR technicians quickly master soldering and brazing techniques.

### Objectives

- [ ] **1.** 描述并演示安全锡焊铜管的过程
  - Describe and demonstrate the safe process of soldering copper tubing.
  - [ ] a. 描述并演示锡焊铜管所需的 PPE、工具和材料
    - Describe and demonstrate the use of the PPE, tools, and materials needed to solder copper tubing.
  - [ ] b. 描述并演示锡焊前的准备工作
    - Describe and demonstrate the preparation required for soldering.
  - [ ] c. 描述并演示锡焊过程

## 03105 Basic Carbon Steel Piping Practices

碳钢管。学完要能在指导下切割、铰孔并套丝钢管，并用各种管件连接螺纹管或短管节。

### Overview

很多 HVACR 系统会用到铁管和钢管。钢管用来输送水暖系统里的水，也用来给炉子供气。正确切割、套丝和连接钢管，是安装工和维修技术员都要掌握的技能。

> Iron and steel pipe are used in many HVACR systems. Steel pipe is used to carry water in hydronic systems and supply gas to furnaces. The ability to properly cut, thread, and join steel pipe is an important skill for an HVACR installer as well as service technicians.
