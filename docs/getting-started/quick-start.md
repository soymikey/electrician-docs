# Quick Start

## Choose your current textbook

- **NCCER Core, 6th Edition**: shared construction foundations, including safety, math, tools, drawings, and workplace skills.
- **Electrical Level 1, 12th Edition**: eleven Chinese module notes and a module review, arranged by module number.
- **HVACR Level 1, 6th Edition**: nine Chinese module notes and a module review. Follow your instructor when the textbook teaching order differs from the numerical sidebar order.
- **EPA 608 Preparatory Manual**: under HVACR, with Introduction, Core, Type I, Type II, and Type III.

Use the [textbook entries](/docs/) to begin. Core and EPA 608 coverage is described on their individual pages; uploaded notes do not imply every textbook page is included.

## Study a module

1. Preview the objectives and overview before class.
2. Read alongside the lesson and record key English terms and questions.
3. Explain the ideas from memory, then check the notes.
4. Use the Electrical and HVACR module reviews to check objectives. These reviews are not full textbook mock exams.

## Use supplementary material

YouTube notes cover core concepts, US residential circuits, safety and tools, motors and transformers, three-phase and industrial power, and low-voltage electronics. Watch the video, read the notes, and revisit unclear sections.

The **YouTube stage quizzes** contain 20 questions per video topic. They do not assess coverage of Core, Electrical, HVACR, or EPA 608.

Use **Field Trip** for site observations, **Jobsite English** for practical expressions, and **Learning Reference** for formulas and terminology. Current references primarily cover electrical basics.

Textbook collections and supplementary notes remain separate while the course documents are being organized; cross-course links are deferred.
