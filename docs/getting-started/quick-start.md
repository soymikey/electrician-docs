# Quick Start

## Recommended order

1. **Core Concepts**: voltage, current, resistance, power, series circuits, parallel circuits, and closed-loop thinking.
2. **US Residential Circuits**: split-phase 120/240V service, hot, neutral, ground, receptacles, and switching patterns.
3. **Safety and Tools**: breakers, fuses, GFCI, shock hazards, multimeters, clamp meters, and verification habits.
4. **Motors and Transformers**: transformers, generators, batteries, DC motors, and AC induction motors.
5. **Three-Phase and Industrial**: three-phase power, power factor, VFDs, and star-delta starters.
6. **Low-Voltage Electronics**: diodes, rectifiers, capacitors, LEDs, semiconductors, optocouplers, and relays.

## How to study each lesson

- Watch the source video once without pausing.
- Read the lesson notes and vocabulary.
- Rewatch only the parts that still feel fuzzy.
- End each lesson by writing three sentences: what it is, where it appears in the field, and what can become dangerous.
- Use the [Cheatsheet](../formula-reference.md) when a formula, tool, or protection device appears.
- Use the quizzes as a reality check after each stage.
