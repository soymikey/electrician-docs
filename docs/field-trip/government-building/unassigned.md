## IMG_9408 · Overhead services {#photo-9408}

[![Overhead services · IMG_9408](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9408.webp)](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9408.webp)

**What it is**

Several service routes share the ceiling space: electrical conduits, air ducts and other piping.

**Main components**

| Component | Location / evidence | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical conduits | Visible | Protect conductor routes. |
| Ducts and other service piping | Visible; contents require labels | Provide separate transport paths; this is a multi-system layout, not one device. |

**What to look for**

Separate the small metal conduits from the larger ducts and trace visible routes.

**How it works**

Conductors carry electrical energy; fans create pressure differences that move air; pumps create pressure differences that move water. Similar-looking routes can serve very different functions.

## IMG_9444 · AERCO boiler bank {#photo-9444}

[![AERCO boiler bank · IMG_9444](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9444.webp)](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9444.webp)

**What it is**

AERCO Benchmark 1500 boilers are shown with a B-2 tag and an overhead GAS flow label; the manufacturer lists Benchmark as a condensing hot-water boiler series.

**Main components**

| Component | Location / evidence | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Boiler cabinet and control panel | Visible | House equipment and provide operating interfaces. |
| Burner and heat exchanger | Main generic internal components; not photographed | Produce heat and transfer it to water. |
| Gas pipe and flue connections | Visible external services | Supply fuel and discharge combustion products; actual routing requires drawings. |

**What to look for**

Identify the gas pipe, unit IDs, panels and flue connections. Hot-water heating and steam production are different duties.

**How it works**

Combustion heat crosses a heat exchanger into circulating water. If the return water and heat-transfer surfaces are cool enough, water vapor in flue gas can condense and release additional latent heat. A condensing design does not prove that condensation or a particular efficiency occurred at the photographed moment.

Principle references: [AERCO condensing boilers](https://www.aerco.com/products/condensing-boilers).
