**What to look for**

## 2. IMG_9413 · Circulator pumps {#photo-9413}

[![Circulator pumps · IMG_9413](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9413.webp)](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9413.webp)

**What it is**

Small pumps move water around a piping circuit.

**Main components**

| Component | Location / evidence | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Motor housing visible | Converts electrical energy into rotation. |
| Pump casing | Visible at pipe connections | Contains the flow passages and internal rotating parts. |
| Impeller | Typical centrifugal-pump internal part; not visible | Rotates to transfer energy to water. |
| Connected piping | Visible external supporting system | Carries water; it is not an internal pump component. |

**What to look for**

Locate the red and gold pump assemblies, motors, pipe connections and wiring. The pump transports water; the heat source supplies the main heating.

**How it works**

In a typical centrifugal circulator, a motor turns an impeller, transferring mechanical energy to water and creating a pressure difference that overcomes circuit resistance. In a domestic hot-water return loop, circulation can reduce waiting time for hot water.

Principle references: [KSB centrifugal-pump reference](https://configurator.ksbindia.co.in/GeneralUtilities/KSBDocumentation/MISC/Centrifugal_design.pdf).

**Still to confirm**

Confirm the actual circuit and whether the pumps alternate, operate together or provide standby service.

## 3. IMG_9414 · Thermostatic mixing valve assembly {#photo-9414}

[![Thermostatic mixing valve assembly · IMG_9414](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9414.webp)](/img/field-trip/government-building/IMG_9414.webp)

**What it is**

A thermostatic mixing valve blends hot and cold water to control mixed-water outlet temperature.

**Main components**

| Component | Location / evidence | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Valve bodies and ports | Visible central assembly | Contain the mixing mechanism and connect water paths; actual port routing needs confirmation. |
| Temperature adjustment | Visible front control | Sets a target mixed-water temperature; the actual value has not been verified. |
| Thermal element and regulating mechanism | Typical internal structure; not visible | Respond to mixed-water temperature and change the relative hot/cold openings; model details remain unconfirmed. |
| Thermometer and isolation valves | Visible external accessories | Indicate temperature and isolate pipe sections; they are not the internal thermal element. |

**What to look for**

Locate the central valve bodies, temperature adjustment, thermometer, copper connections and HOT WATER RETURN label.

**How it works**

An internal temperature-sensitive element responds to mixed-water temperature and changes the relative hot- and cold-water openings. If the outlet becomes too hot, it reduces the hot-water proportion; if too cool, it acts in the opposite direction. Mixing regulates temperature without producing heat.

Principle references: [Caleffi hydronic and mixing-valve reference](https://www.caleffi.com/sites/default/files/media/external-file/48290_EN.pdf).

**Still to confirm**

Confirm the target temperature, actual port connections and division of duty between the large and small valve assemblies.

## Putting it together: how the group operates

Review the equipment and components above before following these general paths. This is not a verified site schematic or an operating procedure.

### Simplified operating paths

**Typical hot-water branch:** A heat source heats water → hot water is stored → mixing regulates delivery temperature where required → water reaches use points. If a return loop is installed, its pump moves return water toward the heating or storage side; the return pump is not a series step through which all delivered water must pass.

**Typical air branch:** A fan moves replacement air through configured filtration and heat-transfer sections and into ductwork. The section order depends on the unit design.

These are separate learning branches. Their direct connection at this site has not been established.
