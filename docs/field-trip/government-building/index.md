## How to study

1. Open a field group and use its equipment list to recognize the devices.
2. Follow **What it is → Main components → What to look for → How it works** for each photo. Tables distinguish visible parts, generic hidden internals and external accessories.
3. After learning the devices and their components, read **Putting it together** at the bottom of the group. Use further-reading links for related devices.

**Field grouping and system connection are different facts.** Photos in one group are not automatically one water loop, electrical circuit or control system. Different groups may also be connected; drawings or further site information are needed.

## Questions for the instructor

- Group A: Are the hot-water equipment and makeup-air unit connected?
- Group B: What are the exact duties of the Trane cabinet and twin pumps?
- Group C: How do the cabinets, transformer and meters connect on the single-line diagram?
- Group D: Is the vessel a supply pressure tank or a thermal expansion tank?
- Group E: Do the CHWP pumps directly serve this AHU?
- Group F: Where does the glycol circuit connect, including any links to other groups?
