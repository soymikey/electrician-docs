# Main Panel Jobsite Phrases

These phrases come from the video *How To Wire A Main Electrical Panel - Start To Finish!*. Focus on understanding them when a lead electrician or instructor speaks, then practice saying the complete example aloud.

## Quick review

| Jobsite English | Natural Chinese |
|---|---|
| Go ahead and tighten it up. | 接着把它拧紧。 |
| Back out the screw a little bit. | 把螺丝稍微松出来一点。 |
| Slide it into place. | 把它滑到位。 |
| Cut it to length. | 按需要的长度剪好。 |
| Don't cut it too short. | 不要剪得太短。 |
| Strip the casing off. | 把外皮剥掉。 |
| Make sure it is secure. | 确保它已经固定牢靠。 |
| Keep the wires organized. | 把电线整理整齐。 |
| Check your local code. | 查看当地电气规范。 |
| Always double-check your work. | 一定要再次检查你的工作。 |

## Speak it aloud

Read each English sentence three times: slowly, at normal speed, and once without looking. Then cover the English column and try to say the phrase from the Chinese meaning.

## Safety boundary

This page is for language and concept learning. It is not a wiring procedure. Main-panel work can involve lethal voltage and must follow the NEC, local code, permits, inspections, equipment instructions, and qualified supervision.
